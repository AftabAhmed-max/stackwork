Section 05
Logo & Usage Rules
✓   Do
FORGE
  • Champagne wordmark on obsidian — primary lockup
  • Playfair Display is the only permitted font
  • Wide letter-spacing (10px+) is non-negotiable
  • Minimum clear space: 48px all sides
✓   Do (Cream BG)
FORGE
  • Ink-on-cream for packaging and print
  • Never use the champagne color on cream
  • Copper accent line may be added below on print
  • Minimum size: 100px wide for digital
✕   Don't
FORGE
  • Never compress or stretch the wordmark
  • Never alter the tracking — condensed looks cheap
  • Never apply drop shadows or embossing effects
✕   Don't
Forge
  • Never use the body font for the wordmark
  • Mixed case is not permitted under any application
  • Copper is an accent only — not for the primary mark
