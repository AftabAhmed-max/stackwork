Section 05
Logo & Usage
✓ Do
FORGE
  • G accent in Teal on dark background
  • Tight tracking (−2px) is part of the mark
  • Clear space minimum: 40px all sides
  • Never set below 80px wide for digital
✓ Do (Light)
FORGE
  • Night-on-sand for packaging and print use
  • Use Teal Dark (#1E7268) for the G on light bg
  • Teal light variant unusable on white — contrast fails
  • No colour change to the G accent permitted
✕ Don't
FORGE
  • Don't use wide tracking — tight spacing is the mark
  • Don't remove the G accent treatment
  • Don't set in teal only — loses hierarchy
✕ Don't
FORGE
  • Never use light weights — 900 Black only
  • No gradient treatments on the wordmark
  • Don't remove the G colour split — that's the brand
