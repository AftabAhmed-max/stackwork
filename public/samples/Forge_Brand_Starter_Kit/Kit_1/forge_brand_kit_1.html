Section 05
LOGO & USAGE RULES
✓   Do
FORGE
  • Use on dark backgrounds for maximum contrast
  • Maintain minimum 40px clear space on all sides
  • Gold on black is the primary lockup
  • All-caps wordmark only — no lowercase
✓   Do (Light BG)
FORGE
  • Use charcoal on light backgrounds only
  • Never use gold on white — insufficient contrast
  • Light background version for print collateral only
  • Minimum logo size: 80px wide
✕   Don't
FORGE
  • Don't use low-opacity versions of the logo
  • Don't place gold wordmark on white
  • Don't stretch or condense the letterforms
✕   Don't
forge
  • Don't use italic or styled variants
  • Don't change font family under any circumstance
  • Don't use lowercase — defeats brand authority
