STACKWORK BRAND REFERENCE
Official brand identity document for all design and development work.

COMPANY NAME
Stackwork
Written as: Stackwork (not STACKWORK, not stackwork)
Domain: stackworkhq.com
Tagline: Digital Solutions

BRAND COLORS
Primary — Deep Navy
Hex: #0D1B3E
Use for: Logo background, main section backgrounds, navigation, headings
Accent — Electric Orange
Hex: #FF6B35
Use for: All CTA buttons, hover effects, links, the word "work" in logo, icon highlights
Background — Charcoal Black
Hex: #1C1C2E
Use for: Main website background, dark section fills, footer
Success — Forest Green
Hex: #2C6E49
Use for: Success messages, completed project badges, positive stats, growth indicators
Premium — Warm Gold
Hex: #C9A84C
Use for: Premium tier highlights, special badges, elevated UI moments — use sparingly
Secondary — Slate Blue
Hex: #3A5F8A
Use for: Secondary buttons, info cards, supporting UI elements, section breaks
Text on dark backgrounds: #FFFFFF white
Text on light backgrounds: #0D1B3E deep navy
Muted / subtext color: #888888

TYPOGRAPHY
Heading Font: Syne Bold
Used for: All headings H1 through H4, logo wordmark, hero text
Source: Google Fonts — fonts.google.com/specimen/Syne
Weight used: 700 Bold only
Body Font: DM Sans
Used for: All body text, paragraphs, buttons, navigation, labels, captions
Source: Google Fonts — fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans
Weights used: 400 Regular and 500 Medium

LOGO DETAILS
Icon: Three horizontal bars stacked vertically, stepping downward-right like a staircase
Bar 1 top longest: #FF6B35 at 100% opacity
Bar 2 middle: #FF6B35 at 75% opacity
Bar 3 bottom shortest: #FF6B35 at 50% opacity on dark backgrounds, #0D1B3E at 50% opacity on light backgrounds
All bars have slightly rounded corners rx 3 to 3.5
Wordmark: Stack in white on dark backgrounds, Stack in #0D1B3E on light backgrounds, work always in #FF6B35
Tagline below wordmark: DIGITAL SOLUTIONS in uppercase, letter-spaced, muted gray
Primary logo version: icon and wordmark on dark charcoal background #1C1C2E
Inverted logo version: icon and wordmark on white background with navy text

BUTTON STYLES
Primary button: Background #FF6B35, text white, border-radius 6px
Secondary button: Transparent background, border #3A5F8A, text white, border-radius 6px
Hover state on primary: slight darkening of orange, smooth transition

SPACING AND RADIUS
Border radius standard: 8px for components, 12px for cards
Font sizes: H1 48px, H2 36px, H3 24px, H4 20px, Body 16px, Small 13px, Label 11px
